# Dive Into the Ultimate Puzzle: The Inheritance Games Review

If you love high-stakes mysteries, eccentric billionaires, and deadly games of wit, *The Inheritance Games* by Jennifer Lynn Barnes belongs at the top of your reading list. This explosive young adult novel has taken the BookTok community by storm, captivating readers with its relentless pacing and intricate puzzles. 

The story follows Avery Grambs, a regular girl living out of her car who suddenly inherits the multi-billion-dollar fortune of Tobias Hawthorne. The catch? To claim her inheritance, Avery must move into Hawthorne House—a sprawling mansion filled with secret passages, riddles, and traps—and live alongside the late billionaire's bitter, grieving grandsons. Among them are Grayson and Jameson Hawthorne, two enigmatic brothers who are just as determined to solve the puzzle as Avery is.

This book is an absolute must-read for fans of fast-paced mysteries, enemies-to-lovers tension, and *Knives Out* style drama. If you devour locked-room mysteries, secret societies, and smart heroines who outsmart wealthy heirs, you will not be able to put this page-turner down.
