Step into a world of curses, carousels, and immortal tricksters with Stephanie Garber's enchanting novel, *Once Upon a Broken Heart*. Known for her dazzling world-building in the *Caraval* series, Garber returns with a brand new standalone trilogy that captures the whimsy and danger of fairy tales gone wrong. If you love atmospheric settings and slow-burn romantic tension, this book is an absolute must-add to your reading list.

The story follows Evangeline Fox, a hopeless romantic who will do anything to stop the love of her life from marrying another. In a desperate bid, she strikes a bargain with the charismatic and infamous Prince of Hearts, Jacks. But Jacks wants three kisses of Evangeline's choosing, given at a time and place of his choosing. Of course, dealing with an immortal villain never goes according to plan, and Evangeline soon finds herself thrust into a dangerous magical game where happy endings come at a steep price.

This book is tailor-made for fans of Young Adult fantasy romance, especially readers who adore enemies-to-lovers tropes, morally gray love interests, and whimsical, fable-like atmospheres. If you enjoyed series like *The Cruel Prince* or *Caraval*, you will completely devour the lush descriptions and heart-pounding chemistry between Evangeline and Jacks.
