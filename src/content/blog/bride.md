Ali Hazelwood is best known for her STEM-focused contemporary romances, but with *Bride*, she makes a thrilling leap into the paranormal romance genre. The story follows Misery Lark, a vampyre diplomat who is forced into a dangerous arranged marriage with the alpha of the werewolf pack, Lowe Moreland. Set against a backdrop of deep-seated political hostility, this novel blends high stakes with the author's signature witty banter and undeniable romantic tension.

At its core, *Bride* delivers a classic enemies-to-lovers dynamic, complete with a reluctant alliance that slowly transforms into an all-consuming passion. Misery is a fiercely independent and sharp-witted protagonist, while Lowe is the ultimate protective, grumpy-with-only-her alpha. Hazelwood expertly builds a world where supernatural factions are on the brink of war, yet manages to keep the emotional and romantic journey of the main characters at the very center of the narrative.

This book is an absolute must-read for fans of paranormal romance, grumpy-sunshine dynamics, and forced-proximity tropes. If you loved Hazelwood's previous academic romances but are craving a bit more bite—literally—this supernatural crossover will satisfy all your reading cravings. It is also perfect for BookTok enthusiasts looking for their next binge-worthy fantasy romance obsession.
