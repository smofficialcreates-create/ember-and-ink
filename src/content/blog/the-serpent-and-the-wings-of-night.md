If you spend any time on BookTok or within the fantasy romance community, you have undoubtedly heard whispers of Carissa Broadbent’s sensational novel, *The Serpent and the Wings of Night*. This dark, atmospheric tale kicks off the *Crowns of Nyaxia* series and delivers everything a romantasy fan could possibly crave: high stakes, deadly trials, and a slow-burn romance that will completely consume you.

Set in a war-weary vampire kingdom, the story follows Oraya, a human adopted by the nightborn vampire king. To survive in a world that views her as prey, she must enter the Kejari—a legendary, mortal-peril tournament hosted by the goddess of death. But survival demands an uneasy alliance with Raihn, a rival vampire whose lethal charm is matched only by his mysterious motives. As the trials grow deadlier, the line between enmity and desire blurs dangerously.

Broadbent’s world-building is immersive and ruthless, offering a fresh take on vampire lore mixed with the beloved Hunger Games-style tournament trope. Oraya is a fiercely resilient protagonist who refuses to be a damsel, while Raihn brings the perfect blend of danger, devotion, and devastating chemistry. The emotional depth and pacing keep the pages turning long past midnight.

This book is an absolute must-read for fans of Jennifer L. Armentrout, Sarah J. Maas, and anyone who loves high-stakes fantasy with a heavy dose of romance. If you enjoy slow-burn tension, morally grey characters, and heart-pounding action, this belongs at the very top of your TBR pile.
