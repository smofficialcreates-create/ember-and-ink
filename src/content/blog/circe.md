If you fell in love with *The Song of Achilles*, stepping into Madeline Miller's second masterpiece, *Circe*, is an absolute must. Released to critical acclaim, this novel takes one of Greek mythology's most misunderstood minor goddesses and thrusts her into the literary spotlight. Miller crafts a lush, immersive world that breathes fresh life into ancient legends, focusing not on the booming voices of the Olympian gods, but on the quiet, enduring power of an exiled witch.

The story follows Circe, the daughter of the sun god Helios and the ocean nymph Perseota. Born without her father's blinding radiance or her mother's alluring charm, she is an outcast in a glittering, cruel divine court. When she discovers she possesses the forbidden craft of witchcraft—capable of turning rivals into monsters and challenging the gods themselves—she is banished to the desolate island of Aiaia. Yet, what begins as a punishment becomes a profound journey of self-discovery, survival, and fierce independence as she encounters historical figures like Odysseus, Daedalus, and the Minotaur.

This enchanting novel is tailor-made for readers who adore lush prose, feminist retellings, and deeply humanized mythological tales. If you enjoy character-driven fantasy, rich atmospheric world-building, and stories of resilience against impossible odds, *Circe* will capture your heart and refuse to let go. It speaks directly to anyone who has ever felt sidelined, underestimated, or forced to carve out their own path in an unforgiving world.
