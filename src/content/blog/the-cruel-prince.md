Welcome back to the blog, bookworms! If you have spent any time on BookTok or Bookstagram recently, you have undoubtedly heard whispers about Faerie courts, dangerous princes, and ruthless human protagonists. Today, we are diving deep into *The Cruel Prince* by Holly Black, the sensational opening installment of the *Folk of the Air* series. Prepare yourselves, because the world of Elfhame is nothing like the fairy tales you grew up with.

Set in a mesmerizing and treacherous world, the story follows Jude Duarte, a mortal girl who was stolen away as a child and raised in the dangerous High Court of Faerie. Despised by the fae for her mortality, Jude must fight for a place among them, drawing the attention—and the cruel torment—of Prince Cardan, the wickedest youngest son of the High King. To survive, Jude must master deadly politics, outsmart her enemies, and embrace her own capacity for violence and ambition.

*The Cruel Prince* is tailor-made for readers who crave high-stakes fantasy filled with morally gray characters. If you love slow-burn enemies-to-lovers tropes, fierce heroines who aren't afraid to get their hands dirty, and intricate political intrigue without predictable pacing, this book belongs at the very top of your TBR pile immediately.
