Are you struggling to find time for your personal growth amidst the chaos of daily life? In *The 5AM Club*, renowned leadership expert Robin Sharma introduces a revolutionary morning routine designed to help you own your morning and elevate your life. Through a captivating story of an struggling artist, a burnt-out entrepreneur, and a billionaire mentor, Sharma lays out a blueprint for unlocking your highest potential before the rest of the world even wakes up.

At the core of the book is the famous '20/20/20 Formula,' which divides your first hour of the day into three segments: 20 minutes of vigorous exercise, 20 minutes of reflection and journaling, and 20 minutes of learning or personal growth. Sharma emphasizes that mindset is nothing without health, and mastering your mornings is the ultimate catalyst for sustained success, deep focus, and unshakeable resilience against daily distractions.

This book is an absolute must-read for entrepreneurs, creatives, and anyone feeling stuck in a reactive cycle. If you want to stop making excuses, optimize your daily performance, and finally carve out time for your biggest dreams, this is the guide you need. However, it requires serious discipline and a willingness to completely rethink your sleep schedule and evening habits.
