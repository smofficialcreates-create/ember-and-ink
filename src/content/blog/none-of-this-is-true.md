If you are looking for a psychological thriller that will completely consume your weekend, look no further than *None of This Is True* by Lisa Jewell. Known for her masterclass pacing and deeply unsettling characters, Jewell returns with one of her most inventive and addictive plots yet, exploring the dark side of true crime obsession and modern influencer culture.

The story follows popular true-crime podcaster Alix Summer, who crosses paths with an unassuming woman named Josie Fair outside a local pub. As it turns out, the two women share the exact same birthday and were born in the same hospital. When Josie suggests she and Alix would make the perfect subjects for a birthday-themed podcast episode about ordinary women changing their lives, Alix agrees. However, as Alix delves deeper into Josie's seemingly ordinary life, she uncovers a web of dark secrets, erratic behavior, and shocking revelations that suggest Josie is hiding a terrifying past.

This book is an absolute must-read for fans of unreliable narrators, mixed-media storytelling (incorporating podcast transcripts and documentary snippets), and slow-burn mysteries that explode into shocking twists. If you devoured books like *The Silent Patient* or loved Netflix true-crime documentaries, this novel will be right up your alley. It masterfully questions the ethics of storytelling and how much we really know about the people we let into our lives.
