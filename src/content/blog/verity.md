If you thought Colleen Hoover only wrote heartbreaking romance, think again. *Verity* dives deep into the dark, twisted corridors of psychological suspense, leaving readers questioning every single character from the very first chapter. Known for her emotional storytelling, Hoover pivots masterfully into the thriller genre with a book that has taken the reading community by storm.

The story follows Lowen Ashleigh, a struggling writer who accepts a life-changing job offer: completing the remaining books of a successful series by bestselling author Verity Crawford, who has been incapacitated by a tragic accident. When Lowen arrives at the Crawford home to sort through Verity's notes, she uncovers an unpublished, chilling autobiography written by Verity herself—one that reveals horrific truths about the family. As Lowen grows closer to Verity's grieving husband, Jeremy, she realizes that Verity might not be as incapacitated as she lets on.

This book is an absolute must-read for fans of fast-paced psychological thrillers, unreliable narrators, and dark romance elements. If you loved novels like *Gone Girl* or *The Woman in the Window*, *Verity* belongs at the very top of your TBR pile. It is especially suited for readers who enjoy morally gray characters and jaw-dropping plot twists that linger long after turning the final page.
