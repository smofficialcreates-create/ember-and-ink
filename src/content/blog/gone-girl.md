If you thought you knew what a toxic marriage looked like, Gillian Flynn’s modern masterpiece, *Gone Girl*, is here to completely shatter your perspective. Published in 2012, this psychological thriller took the literary world by storm, introducing readers to one of the most unreliable, chilling, and fascinating couples in modern fiction: Nick and Amy Dunne. 

The story kicks off on the morning of their fifth wedding anniversary when Amy mysteriously disappears from their rented home in North Carthage, Missouri. As a media circus descends upon the town, the police quickly turn their attention to Nick. Through alternating diary entries from Amy and current perspectives from Nick, Flynn peels back the layers of a deeply dysfunctional marriage, revealing a labyrinth of deceit, resentment, and shocking twists that will leave your jaw on the floor.

*Gone Girl* is an absolute must-read for fans of dark, gritty mysteries and complex character studies. It is especially suited for readers who love morally grey protagonists, unreliable narrators, and plots that keep you guessing until the very last page. If you enjoyed books like *The Girl on the Train* or *The Silent Patient*, this belongs at the very top of your reading list immediately.
